# What works today

Each part of the project, with its report. The [README](../README.md) has the summary.

- **75 filings, pinned and checked:** the last three 10-Ks of 25 companies, pinned by accession number, split into sections and 42,612 passages, and checked by `make verify-data` ([data report](data_report.md), [spot check](spot_check.md), [week 1 summary](week1_summary.md)).
- **Hybrid search, measured:** vector, keyword and hybrid search over the passages, scored on 40 questions with and without a company and year filter. Hybrid search did not beat vector search on these questions, and the report says so ([retrieval report](retrieval_report.md)).
- **Baseline answers with citations:** plain retrieval-augmented answers from one model call, asked to cite every claim to its passage; an answer with an uncited figure is flagged. They are not checked, and on the example trap questions they guess the company or the year, or decline to answer ([baseline answers](baseline_examples.md)).
- **The API:** a FastAPI service that answers or streams questions, keeps a request log, and limits questions per address and per day; it runs in a Docker image ([filingcheck/app](../filingcheck/app/)).
- **The web page:** a Next.js page that asks the agent with its checks on and shows each tool step as it runs, a chip for each cited passage or number (a number's chip opens to the value as filed, its period and the SEC document), a "What was checked" panel, and seven saved example answers ([web](../web/)).
- **The numbers and the agent:** company totals from each filing's own XBRL facts, cross-checked against the SEC's companyfacts; three tools with typed inputs (`search_filings`, `get_financial_fact`, `calculate`); and an agent that chooses between them and cites the passages and facts it used. It runs on the page, from the command line (`make ask MODE=agent`) and from the API (`mode: "agent"`) ([facts report](facts_report.md), [metric coverage](facts_coverage.md), [week 3 summary](week3_summary.md)).
- **Skeptical checks on the tools:** before `get_financial_fact` runs, five checks compare its arguments with the question's words and the SEC data: the company, the fiscal year (including "last year"), the metric and period, the units and the segment. Before `search_filings` runs, the fiscal-year check does the same for its year, and sends a search that names no year to the company's latest filing. Each passes the call, repairs it when the question settles the answer, or sends it back to the model with the options, once. Every decision, passes included, goes into an override log that is kept with each answer, in the request log and in the evaluation rows ([week 4 summary](week4_summary.md)).
- **Graders:** a deterministic grader for numbers, and an LLM grader for text answers and for a second opinion on number grades (on the failed ones in the development runs, on every number-graded run in the final run) ([eval](../eval/README.md)). The LLM grader was checked on two samples of 40 stored answers, each graded blind by other automatic graders. On the second sample, after one fix to its prompt, it agreed with the first blind grader on 37 of 40 verdicts; on a later sample of 20 text answers, 16 of 20. In the final run, 30 text verdicts drawn by seed were graded blind by two other automatic graders: the LLM grader agreed with them on 28 and 27 of 30. The owner reviewed the grades on ten rows.
- **Two sealed held-out trap files** (112 questions, 110 scored; `eval/questions/heldout_traps.jsonl` and `heldout_traps_2.jsonl`). Both were sealed by SHA-256 before the final run and run once from the tagged commit.
- **The measurement:** a pre-registration (`eval/PREREGISTRATION.md`) merged before the first final row was written; five arms on five question sets, 3 runs each (3,855 rows); `make report` computes every number from the committed rows ([full report](../eval/results/2026-11-14_final_report.md)).
- **Number labels:** every figure in an answer is labeled Verified (it is in a cited fact or passage), Conflict (it differs from the cited fact) or Unverified, after the model answers and without changing what it wrote; the page shows the label next to each figure ([labels](../filingcheck/labels/), [week 5 summary](week5_summary.md)).
- **A deterministic gate in CI:** 24 scripted scenarios run the real agent, checks, lookup and grader with no model, and fail the build if a check's decision changes (`make eval-smoke`, [eval](../eval/README.md)).
